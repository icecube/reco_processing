# step2_hese_flavor_reunblind / studies

Side studies for the step2 (flavor) reunblinding fit.

## compare_SAY_Poisson.ipynb
Compares the nominal 1D scan with the SAY likelihood (`nbestfit50_all_param_2sigma_1D_10steps_PassingHESE`) to the same scan with the Poisson likelihood (`..._PassingHESE_Poisson`). It shows the best fits and 1σ errors of all parameters side by side, overlays the profile likelihoods and plots an overview of the shifts (`plots_compare_SAY_Poisson/`).
Poisson gives the same astro_norm (3.59 vs 3.62), a slightly softer spectrum (γ 2.85 vs 2.93, ~0.5σ) and a lower νe ratio (0.72 vs 0.90, ~0.6σ), with the largest nuisance shifts in the muon parameters; everything stays within 1σ. The notebook also prints a 2-column LaTeX table (SAY vs Poisson) and compares the data flavor triangle of the 3 combined SAY scans with the Poisson scan `nbestfit50_Poisson`: Poisson moves the best fit from 0.472 : 0.527 : 0.001 to 0.403 : 0.560 : 0.037 (νe : νμ : ντ) with contours of similar size.

## asimov_closure.ipynb
Makes flavor triangles from the Asimov flavor scans in `dag_scans/.../asimov_closure` (SAY and Poisson with/without systematics and with different muon templates, plus SAY variants with another minimizer and the old simulation), injected at the HESE best fit with a 1:1:1 flavor composition. It marks the injected and best-fit compositions in the triangles, overlays SAY against Poisson in one triangle per setup (with systematics, without, and without with the paper muon template), and prints, for every parameter, the injected value against the best fit (`plots_asimov_closure/`).
Poisson closes exactly everywhere, while the SAY bias depends on the muon template: astro_norm +0.30 / muon norm −0.30 with the combined template, +0.04 / −0.13 with the paper KDE, and closure within +0.001 / −0.02 with the non-paper KDE (`muonconvneha`), independent of the minimizer (LBFGSB vs LBFGSB_force), the simulation version and the NNMFit version; the scan with the old simulation, NNMFit v0.3.0 and default livetime reproduces the earlier benchmark `hese/SAY/hese_HESEBestfit_SPL_noinel_nosyst` (best fit within 0.002, |ΔTS| ≤ 0.16); in those earlier benchmarks (hese_SPL and hese_HESEBestfit_SPL) SAY closes to within 0.01 of Poisson with nearly identical contours.
With the corrected muon template error (`_newmuon`: `template_fluctuation` as a 1σ error instead of `up - low` = 2σ, which takes the combined template from Σσ² = 8.54 to 2.18 events², see `muon_template_kde.ipynb`) the SAY bias falls by a factor ~3, with systematics and without: astro_norm +0.080 / muon norm −0.100 against +0.304 / −0.303, composition 0.328 : 0.342 : 0.330 against 0.318 : 0.364 : 0.319, matching the −0.104 the toy fit of `muon_template_kde.ipynb` predicted. It is not closure: a 12.6-event template with a per-bin σ/μ of order 0.3 still moves the muon norm by 0.1 against its `prior_width = 0.5` prior. The two new Poisson scans stay exact, as they must — on Asimov the injection and the fit use the same template. `compare_SAY_muonsigma[_nosyst]` overlay the old and corrected σ directly: same contour size, shifted best fit.
The contours use all scan points (`analysis_utils.load_triangle_scan(..., drop_unsuccessful_points=False)`): `FlavourScansPlotting.get_contour_points` always drops fits flagged as failed, which with NNMFit ≥ v0.5.0 (v0.3.0 hardcoded `success = 1`) removes up to ~45% of the SAY points (`ABNORMAL_TERMINATION_IN_LNSRCH`), although these failed fits lie on the Poisson profile as well as the converged ones; with all points the earlier SAY and Poisson contours coincide, as in `presentations/2026-03-12_diffuse_update/compare_likelihood_hese.png`.

## muon_template_kde.ipynb
Follows up the SAY closure problem of `asimov_closure.ipynb` by comparing the muon template KDEs themselves: Neha's KDE as it sits on `/data/ana` (`.../override/MuonGun/Tracks_KDE.pickle`, the default `template_file` of `muontemplate_hese` in `components.yaml`, used by no scan here), our rebuild of it (`reproduce_neha`, the non-paper KDE of the `muonconvneha` scans), the paper KDE, and the two templates of `muontemplate_combined_hese` combined as `CombinedTemplateFlux` does at norm 1 and asymmetry 0, both as the first closure scans used them and in the corrected `_sigma` version of the `_newmuon` scans. It tabulates and maps the per-bin expectation, the `template_fluctuation` and the quantities SAY actually uses (sigma/mu and sigma^2/mu), shows how much of the HESE Tracks `ssq` the muon template owns (from the per-component histograms of `../graphs/nbestfit50_all_param_2sigma_1D_10steps_PassingHESE`), derives the single-bin SAY pull on a norm and reproduces the closure bias with a 2-parameter toy SAY fit over the 230 Tracks bins (`plots_muon_template_kde/`).
The muon template is 19% of the Tracks expectation but 97% of the total sigma^2: without it sigma^2/mu stays below 0.01 per bin and SAY is indistinguishable from Poisson, with it the down-going bins below ~200 TeV reach sigma^2/mu = 0.54. Both template generations are read off the same best-fit graph: for a template component ssq/mu^2 is the per-bin relative template error (f/T)^2, independent of the fitted norm and asymmetry, so rescaling the muon ssq with f/T of the corrected pickles is exact (asserted against the stored fluctuations for the generation the fit used). With the corrected template the Tracks sum(sigma^2) falls 2.70 → 0.76 events^2, the muon share 97% → 89%, the largest sigma^2/mu 0.54 → 0.16, and the mean sigma^2/mu over the 88 filled bins 0.029 → 0.0087 against a no-muon floor of 0.0015. The per-bin relative error of all three KDEs is of order 1 (median sigma/mu 1.97, 1.66, 0.95), so what changed is the normalisation: 0.37 → 1.44 → 12.6 muon events in 13.06 yr (raw `MuonWeight`, the paper's tagged-muon scaling, and `MuonWeightCombinedConv/Prompt` scaled to 6.3 events each), which raises sum(sigma^2) from 0.03 to 0.31 to 8.5 events^2. For a single bin SAY shrinks a norm to `1/(1+(sigma/mu)^2)`, which the toy fit reproduces exactly when the muon prior is removed (0.34, 0.42, 0.59) and which would bias the *non-paper* KDE most of the three (the `/data/ana` template, which no scan used, would go further still, to 0.20); with the `prior_width = 0.5` prior the ordering flips, because the pull against the prior scales with the absolute number of muon events. The toy then matches the real scans (muon norm −0.016, −0.11, −0.31 against −0.022, −0.135, −0.303), i.e. it is the absolute size of the muon template, not its relative uncertainty, that broke closure, and the earlier templates were never unbiased, only prior-dominated.
The `/data/ana` template is not the same as our rebuild of it: it holds 1.77 muon events in 13.06 yr against 0.37, a factor 4.8 in rate, and sum(sigma^2) = 2.03 against 0.029 events^2, i.e. nearly as much MC variance as the *corrected* combined template (2.18) out of a seventh of the events; its per-bin sigma/mu is the worst of the four (median 2.8, N_eff = 1.5) and the toy fit puts the muon-norm pull it would give at -0.23. Its `description` field is the unfilled placeholder `"KDE for MuonGun generated using ####"`, so its provenance is not recorded.
The corrected combined template (`_sigma`) is the one controlled comparison in the notebook: same MC, same weights, same 12.6 events, only the error definition changed, and sum(sigma^2) falls 8.54 → 2.18 events^2, median per-bin sigma/mu 0.95 → 0.46, N_eff 18.6 → 73, with no muon event left in a bin with sigma^2/mu > 0.5 (72% before) and N* 0.59 → 0.85. The ratio is 3.92 rather than exactly 4 because the rebuild also seeded the bootstrap, so per bin the sigma ratio scatters between 1.5 and 3.6 around a median of 2.14 and the central template moved by up to 0.53 of its new sigma. The toy fit gives a muon-norm pull of −0.104 for it against the scan's −0.100.
Two follow-ups are recorded in the notebook: `template_fluctuation` is stored as `up - low`, i.e. 2x the bootstrap std summed linearly over the fine KDE grid (`FlavorGlobalFit/muon_template_hese/kde_utils.py`, and the same code in `notebooks/muons/hese_muons_paper.ipynb` and `hese_muons_kde.ipynb`), while NNMFit squares it as a sigma (`core/histogram_builder.py`), so the sigma^2 entering SAY is ~4x too large in every template (since corrected for the combined template, see the `_newmuon` scans above); and `IC86_pass2_SnowStorm_FTP_HESE_Combined.cfg` excludes `muontemplate_hese` and `muontemplate_prompt_hese` but not `muontemplate_combined_hese`.

## analysis_histograms.ipynb
Draws 2D heatmaps of the MC expectation in the analysis binning (cascades, tracks, double cascades next to each other) for the latest best fit (`..._fixedprior_newmuon`: fixed detector priors, new muon template), with and without the SnowStorm gradient shift. It counts per channel the bins with an MC expectation of exactly 0 and below 0 and stores them in `plots_analysis_histograms/bin_counts.csv`.
No bin is negative; with the SnowStorm shift some bins are clipped to exactly 0 (4/230 cascades, 6/230 tracks, 13/130 double cascades), mostly at high energy and in the up-going region, while without the shift no bin is 0.

## compare_tight_priors.ipynb
Compares the step2 fit with the SAY likelihood, the Poisson likelihood, and the Poisson likelihood with 5x tighter SnowStorm detector priors (`..._Poisson_tightpriors` / `nbestfit50_Poisson_tightpriors`), for the 1D profile scans (signal and all-parameter profiles, error overview) and for the data flavor triangle. It prints 3-column best-fit tables (pandas and LaTeX) for both the 1D scans and the flavor triangle.
The tight-prior scans are numerically identical to the default Poisson scans (same llh 92.2737 to 1e-9, same best fit and profiles, also in the flavor triangle), so the changed SnowStorm `prior_width` does not reach the fit: NNMFit (`core/analysis_config.py`, `get_param_priors`) only reads detector priors from a key `prior`, while `SnowStorm_systematics.cfg` uses `priors`, so no detector priors are applied in any of the "wpriors" fits.

## compare_fixedprior.ipynb
Compares the step2 results after fixing the detector priors (`..._fixedprior`, `prior` key): a SAY table with HESE-7.5, HESE-7.5 flavor, HESE-12 flavor, this analysis without detector priors and with fixed priors, a SAY vs Poisson table with fixed priors (pandas and LaTeX), and the data flavor triangle without (3 fine scans combined) and with fixed priors for SAY (`nbestfit50_fixedprior_0`) and Poisson (`nbestfit50_Poisson_fixedprior_0`) (`plots_compare_fixedprior/`).
With the priors astro_norm drops from 3.62 to 3.22, γ from 2.93 to 2.86 and the νe ratio from 0.90 to 0.75 (composition 0.472 : 0.527 : 0.001 → 0.418 : 0.561 : 0.020), the detector parameters move off their range edges (ice_crystal 0.80 → 0.92, ice_abs 1.09 → 1.03), and SAY and Poisson now agree on the signal (astro_norm 3.22 vs 3.19, γ 2.86 vs 2.85, νe ratio 0.745 vs 0.740), also in the flavor triangle (0.418 : 0.561 : 0.020 vs 0.410 : 0.554 : 0.036, nearly identical contours).

## compare_newmuon.ipynb
Compares the step2 fit with fixed detector priors to the same fit with the new muon template (`..._fixedprior_newmuon`, `override/muon/muontemplate_combined_hese_sigma.yaml`: 1σ instead of 2σ template uncertainty, same rate): a SAY table with HESE-7.5, HESE-7.5 flavor, HESE-12 flavor, SAY fixed priors and SAY new muon, a SAY vs Poisson table for the new muon template (pandas and LaTeX), and the data flavor triangle for SAY fixed priors, SAY new muon and Poisson new muon (`plots_compare_newmuon/`).
The SAY signal moves little (astro_norm 3.22 → 3.14, γ 2.860 → 2.859, νe ratio 0.745 → 0.766, composition 0.418 : 0.561 : 0.020 → 0.425 : 0.555 : 0.021) while the SAY muon norm rises from 0.49 to 0.62, closer to Poisson (0.72); SAY and Poisson agree on the signal (astro_norm 3.14 vs 3.18, νe ratio 0.766 vs 0.741) with nearly identical flavor-triangle contours.
